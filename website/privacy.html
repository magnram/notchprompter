Privacy policy

Last updated: 23 September 2026

NotchPrompter is made to keep your words to yourself. In short: we collect nothing.

Your scripts

Scripts are saved only on your Mac, in the app's own folder. They are never sent to us or anyone else. When you import or export a file, the app only reads or writes the file you choose.

Microphone and speech

When you turn on voice-follow, NotchPrompter listens to the microphone and turns your speech into words, to find your place in the script. Voice-follow never saves audio, and the words are only used while you read.

Speech recognition is done by Apple's Speech framework, on your Mac. By default NotchPrompter never sends your speech anywhere. macOS only recognises speech on the Mac when Dictation is turned on in System Settings → Keyboard. If Dictation is off, or your Mac can't recognise a language on its own, you can choose to allow Apple's servers instead. Then your speech is sent to Apple to be turned into text, under Apple's privacy policy. We never receive it.

Camera and recordings

NotchPrompter uses the camera only when you press record. It films you with the camera and microphone and saves the movie in the NotchPrompter folder in your Movies folder. Recordings are never uploaded, and we never receive them. You can delete them in Finder like any other file.

You can turn off camera, microphone and speech access at any time in System Settings → Privacy & Security.

Analytics, ads and accounts

There are none. The app has no account, no analytics, no ads and no tracking, and it does not connect to any server of ours.

Children

The app collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.

Contact

Questions? Open an issue on GitHub. NotchPrompter is open source, so you can also read exactly what it does in the source code.
