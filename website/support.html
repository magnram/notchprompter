Support

Help for NotchPrompter on the Mac and iPhone.

Getting started

  1. Open NotchPrompter. The prompter appears at the top of your screen, under the camera.
  2. Click the red microphone and allow microphone and speech recognition access.
  3. Read your script out loud. The text follows your voice.

Hover over the prompter to see its buttons: back to top, microphone, play, pencil (editor) and settings.

The text doesn't follow my voice

  • Check that the bars next to the notch move when you talk. If not, choose the right microphone in System Settings → Sound → Input.
  • Open Settings in the prompter and check the Voice language. "Automatic" picks the language of your script.
  • If you went off script, click the word you are on. The prompter continues from there.
  • Allow access in System Settings → Privacy & Security → Microphone, and → Speech Recognition.

It asks me to turn on Dictation

NotchPrompter recognises speech on your Mac, so your voice never leaves it. macOS only allows this when Dictation is on: System Settings → Keyboard → Dictation. If you'd rather not turn it on, choose Use Apple's Servers, or turn off Recognise speech on this Mac only in Settings → Privacy.

How do I record myself?

Hover over the prompter and click the record button (the circle), or press C. After a 3-2-1 countdown it films you with the camera and microphone while the text follows your voice. Click it again to stop. Each take is saved in Movies › NotchPrompter. To record the screen, or the screen and the camera together, choose it under Record in Settings → Recording.

I can't see the prompter in my screen recording

That is on purpose, so your audience can't see it. To show it, turn off Hide the prompter from screen sharing and recordings in Settings.

How do I change the size?

Drag the left, right or bottom edge of the prompter, or a bottom corner. Text size is in the settings popover (the slider button), or press ⌘ + and ⌘ −.

Can I add notes to myself?

Yes. Put them in parentheses or brackets, like (pause) or [smile]. The prompter shows them in smaller italics, and voice-follow doesn't wait for you to say them. It moves past them as you read on.

Where are my scripts?

Click the pencil in the prompter, or choose File → Edit Scripts. They are saved automatically on your Mac. To keep a copy, right-click a script and choose Export.

Keyboard shortcuts

Click the prompter first, then:

  • Space play or pause
  • V follow my voice on or off
  • C start or stop recording
  • R back to the top
  • ↑ ↓ faster or slower
  • ← → narrower or wider
  • E open the editor
  • Esc stop

NotchPrompter for iPhone

Pick a script and tap Open in Prompter. The script sits at the top of the screen, under the front camera. Tap the microphone to follow your voice, or choose Scroll and tap play. The Aa button sets the text size and speed. The red button records a take, which is saved to your Photos.

What is free on iPhone, and how do I restore my purchase?

Scroll mode, the editor and the practice script are always free. With your own scripts, you get 3 free takes with voice-follow and recording. After that, a one-time purchase unlocks everything. There is no subscription. If you bought it before, open Settings (the gear) and tap Restore Purchase, signed in with the same Apple Account.

Still stuck?

Open an issue on GitHub and describe what happened. Include your macOS or iOS version and your script's language.
