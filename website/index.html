Questions

Is it really free?

Yes. NotchPrompter is free and open source under the MIT license. No ads, no subscription, no account. The source code is on GitHub, where you can report bugs, suggest features or help build it.

Does it work without a notch?

Yes. On a Mac without a notch the prompter hangs from the top of the screen, under the menu bar, which is still close to the camera.

Which languages does voice-follow support?

Dozens, including English, Norwegian, German, French, Spanish, Chinese and Japanese. NotchPrompter sees which language your script is in and listens for that language, so you don't have to set anything. You can pick another language in Settings.

Will people see the prompter when I share my screen?

No. By default it is hidden from screen sharing, screenshots and recordings. You can turn this off in Settings if you want to show it.

Does anything go to the cloud?

No. Speech is recognised on your Mac (with Dictation turned on in System Settings), and NotchPrompter has no servers. Voice-follow never saves audio. Only when you press record is a movie saved, in your own Movies folder. Read the privacy policy.

What are the keyboard shortcuts?

Click the prompter, then: Space play/pause, V voice, C record, R back to top, ↑ ↓ speed, + − text size, E editor, Esc stop.
