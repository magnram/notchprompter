Skip a sentence, change a word, say "uh". It finds where you are and jumps ahead with you. Pause, and it waits.

Smooth scrolling with a 3-2-1 countdown. Hover to pause, and use the tortoise and the hare to set the speed.

Keep all your scripts in one place, or as Markdown files in a folder that any editor or AI assistant can use. Import Word, RTF or text files.

Record yourself

Questions

Is it really free?

Yes. NotchPrompter for Mac is free and open source under the MIT license. No ads, no subscription, no account. The source code is on GitHub, where you can report bugs, suggest features or help build it.

NotchPrompter for iPhone is free too, with no watermark. If you want to support the project, you can get Pro, which adds more video and editing tools. Read more.

Does it work without a notch?

Yes. On a Mac without a notch the prompter hangs from the top of the screen, under the menu bar, which is still close to the camera.

Which languages does voice-follow support?

Dozens, including English, Norwegian, German, French, Spanish, Chinese and Japanese. NotchPrompter sees which language your script is in and listens for that language, so you don't have to set anything. You can pick another language in Settings.

Will people see the prompter when I share my screen?

No. By default it is hidden from screen sharing, screenshots and recordings. You can turn this off in Settings if you want to show it.

Does anything go to the cloud?

No. Speech is recognised on your Mac (with Dictation turned on in System Settings), and NotchPrompter has no servers. Voice-follow never saves audio. Takes are saved only in your own Movies folder. If you use iCloud, your scripts sync through your own iCloud account. Read the privacy policy.

What are the keyboard shortcuts?

Click the prompter, then: Space play/pause, V voice, C record, R back to top, ↑ ↓ speed, + − text size, E editor, Esc stop.

In any app: ⌃⌥P show or hide the prompter, ⌃⌥V pause or go on, ⌃⌥R record.
