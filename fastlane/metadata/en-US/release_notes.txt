• Your scripts now sync through iCloud with the new NotchPrompter app for iPhone.
• Scripts folder: keep every script as a Markdown file in a folder you choose, so any editor or AI assistant can write them.
• Transcripts: every take gets a subtitle file (.srt), made on your Mac.
• Shortcuts that work in any app: ⌃⌥P shows or hides the prompter, ⌃⌥V pauses it, ⌃⌥R records.
• Choose which screen the prompter is on. It now follows when you close the lid or plug in a display.
• Screen light: fill the screen with a soft light on your face during a take, or with black against glare.
• A new menu beside the record button: camera, microphone with its level, recording mode and screen light.
• Play the last take right away, or move it to the Trash for a quick retake.
• Voice-follow marks the end of the script, and Settings now has tabs.
